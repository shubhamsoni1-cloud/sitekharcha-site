Site ka kharcha, ek jagah.

SiteKharcha is a cashbook for contractors and interior firms: expenses by site, money received, vendor and client dues, GST bills, partner accounts and labour hazri. In Hindi and English, with voice entry.

  • Every expense and receipt, by site and by head
  • Site-wise profit and what clients and vendors owe
  • Labour hazri, advances, payday and monthly statements on WhatsApp
  • Partners on their own phones, synced
  • Bol ke entry: “Ramesh ko 18500 diye Baner site”
